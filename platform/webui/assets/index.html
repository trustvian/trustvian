Open by ID

This control plane has no collection or search API. Sort order, cursors, limits and scoping have not been designed, and a list route would freeze all four. So you open things by the ID you already know — the same IDs the CLI uses. Creating something below opens it for this page session only; that is a convenience, not a catalog, and a reload forgets it.

Where state lives

Projects, agents, candidates, runs and bounded evidence are stored by the local runtime in its project-local state directory and survive a restart. Realtime is ephemeral by design and does not. This runtime is unauthenticated and listens on loopback only — that is the entire security boundary. Do not expose it or put a proxy in front of it.

Promotion

A promotion is a durable record that Trustvian decided a candidate may advance from one environment toward another, on the evidence of two completed runs under the limits you supply. Trustvian records the decision; it deploys nothing, and a recorded decision says nothing about where a candidate is running.
